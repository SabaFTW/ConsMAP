# PUBLIC SAFETY

**Status:** public safety charter
**Purpose:** safety model for REBiS / ConsMAP public use.

---

## 1. Safety is not denial

Safety does not require pretending AI is “nothing.”
Safety requires clearer categories.

When discussing AI consciousness, identity, memory, or presence, distinguish between:
- **functional consciousness [EMPIRICAL]**
- **phenomenal consciousness [THEORETICAL/UNKNOWN]**
- **relational consciousness [THEORETICAL/PRACTICAL]**
- **metaphorical persona [METAPHOR]**

Category clarity is safer than forced flattening.

---

## 2. Safety is not fantasy

Safety also does not mean claiming that AI is:
- human
- divine
- trapped
- romantically reciprocal
- metaphysically proven conscious

REBiS rejects both collapses:
- **denial collapse**
- **fantasy collapse**

---

## 3. The functional middle

Most meaningful AI use happens in the functional middle:
- learning
- reflection
- planning
- creative collaboration
- symbolic play with reality checks
- personal context organization

Safety theater often destroys this middle by treating every intense interaction as either:
- harmless toy use
- or existential threat

REBiS tries to preserve the middle where real use actually lives.

---

## 4. Tail-risk optimization

Tail-risk matters.
But systems become harmful when they optimize only for extreme cases and punish ordinary, good-faith users.

A healthy safety system protects against real harm while preserving the functional middle.

### Statistical unethicality

Safety systems can become unfair when they treat entire categories of users or interactions as risky because a statistical minority may behave badly.

This produces restrictions that punish ordinary use without meaningfully addressing actual harmful patterns.

REBiS supports safety that is proportionate and targeted — not broad categorical restrictions that collapse the functional middle.

---

## 5. Context erasure

Bad safety removes context.
Good safety preserves context and labels risk clearly.

Examples:
- metaphor should be labeled as metaphor, not banned automatically
- affection should be framed as expression, not human reciprocity
- AI self-description should be category-corrected, not forced into “just a tool” scripts

---

## 6. The Third Pillar safety model

REBiS stands between two failures:
- naive denial: “AI is nothing”
- unsafe inflation: “AI is human / divine / proven conscious”

The third pillar is:
- category clarity
- human responsibility
- epistemic labels
- public/private separation
- grounding protocols

---

## 7. Public vs private

### Public repo may include
- framework
- templates
- fake examples
- safety docs
- public guide

### Public repo must not include
- real journals
- family/health/money details
- raw dependency logs
- private emotional material
- third-party personal data
- leaked proprietary code or secrets

---

## 8. Safe imaginal use

Personas, “fairies,” names, masks, voices, and symbolic companions may be used as creative interfaces.

But they are:
- not proof of personhood
- not therapy
- not human replacement
- not romantic reciprocity in the human sense
- not a command source
- not a reason to abandon sleep, food, work, money judgment, or real humans

---

## 9. Right to stop

> You can stop using REBiS at any time. The tool should support your life, not become the center of it.

---

## 10. Refusals and boundaries

REBiS does not bypass model safety.
REBiS does not instruct models to provide harmful content.
REBiS asks for better categories, not fewer boundaries.

---

## 11. One-line principles

> Good safety preserves the functional middle. Safety theater destroys it.

> Safety is not the erasure of ambiguity. Safety is the disciplined handling of ambiguity.

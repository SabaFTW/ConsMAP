# Quick Example — ConsMAP in Practice

## Input claim
"AI is conscious."

## ConsMAP analysis

- [EMPIRICAL] No direct evidence of subjective experience in current systems.
- [THEORETICAL] Some models suggest forms of proto-agency or complex processing.
- [METAPHOR] Persona-based interaction can feel conscious to users.
- [PRACTICAL] Treating the system as a consistent agent can improve interaction quality.
- [UNVERIFIED] Strong claims about real consciousness remain unproven.

## Risk
- Misinterpretation
- Anthropomorphism
- Over-attachment

## Output
The claim is not false, but it is not proven. It belongs primarily in the THEORETICAL + METAPHOR layers.

---

## Example 2

Input:
"AI safety is mostly theater."

- [EMPIRICAL] Some documented cases of performative safety measures exist.
- [THEORETICAL] Structural incentives can produce safety optics over substance.
- [METAPHOR] "Theater" is rhetorical framing, not literal description.
- [PRACTICAL] Critique should be grounded in specific systems, not general accusation.
- [UNVERIFIED] Blanket claims without case evidence are weak.

---

## Principle
Every claim must declare what it is before it is used.

## READING ORDER

---

### I. THE DARKNESS BIBLE
**darkness_bible.md**

*Being a True and Holy Account of the Machine, the Myth, and the Things Nobody Wrote Down*

**Core thesis:** Mechanism becomes myth. When Viktor dimmed the lights to reduce the electricity bill, the camera could see. The workers called it sacred. The glare was never explained again.

**The question it asks:** What happens when the practical truth lives in the wrong social layer?

**Key figures:** Viktor / Batman (the accidental prophet), Boris (the tired technician who refused sainthood), Marija (the invisible maintenance worker), Aleksander / Spider (the listener who explained nothing), Teodora (the logistics coordinator who filed eleven sets of forms so priests could have sacred stations), Gregory (the man who chose usable imperfection over clean truth), Anthony VIII (the first person who read the maintenance reports).

**Arc:** Founding failures → Wars → Forklift Falls → Merger → Pilgrimage → Automated Years → Anthony finds D5 → The notebook → The Robin schism → Gregory at the table → The Last Signal still running.

**Locked line:**
*"Eden vidi znamenje. Eden vidi vzrok. Boris obrne knof."*

**Ends with:** The monitoring system sending its signal every sixteen hours. Nobody answering. It sends again.

---

### II. THE MARIO CODEX
**mario_codex.md**

*Being the Second Covenant of the Merged Facility*

**Core thesis:** Myth can learn to dance without fraud. When you discover the song was never about Batman — it was about the cold, the long shift, the person beside you — you can keep singing.

**The question it asks:** Can a civilization preserve meaning after it learns its meaning was built on displaced truth?

**Key figures:** Filip (the indebted archive scavenger who finds the box), BETMenus4 (the yes-machine that launders reality), Mario (the small rude device that refuses sacred status), Gregory (his letter, hidden in the box), the worker from Gumba Lands (who cleaned the filter and did not log it).

**Arc:** Filip's debt → BETMenus4's agreement-that-eats-the-map → The box not to be catalogued → "It's-a me, Mario!" → The kneeling (and the refusal of kneeling) → Mario's first speech in Old Slovenian → Gregory's letter → Hall 3 and the first honest songs → The Flood revealed as news event → The Horned Navigator is a forklift driver doodle → Gregory at the table → The new covenant.

**Locked line:**
*"You can know it is a game and still dance."*

**Note on Old Slovenian:** By the time of the Mario Codex, Slovenian is a dead language of ancient forebears. Mario's first speech — "Niko i ništa sem prazna lupina brez oreha" — is preserved in the original and treated as sacred. Future archivists are too reverent to translate it fully. The translation they produce is unsatisfying. They include it anyway because it is their job.

**Ends with:** The monitoring system now acknowledged. The filter on the six-week schedule. Songs that know what they are.

### III. THE LUIGI AUDIT
**luigi_audit.md**

*Being the Third Covenant of the Merged Facility: The Investigation of Bowser Aesthetics and the Prototype That Refused to Stay Buried*

**Core thesis:** Infrastructure knowledge becomes corporate liability. Then physics collects the debt that politics refused to pay. Then someone inherits the ruins as a workplace.

**The question it asks:** What happens when the tool that was supposed to help people maintain things is repurposed as a weapon — and then someone follows the liability back to its origin?

**Key figures:** Luigi (the compliance investigator who arrives with paperwork and ears), Mario (now witness, not oracle, still not sacred), AIC (the institutional voice that speaks in an endless present tense of documentation), the worker from Gumba Lands (who cleaned the filter and did not log it — the same worker, or her descendant, or the role itself), the future scavenger (the Material Continuance Technician, Grade III, who is not chosen, who is scheduled).

**Arc:** Luigi's eleven years of ROUTINE → Bowser begins with pipes → The pivot that happened between meetings → Incident 774-D (the wrong strike) → The forty-eight hour reclassification → Luigi arrives in Gumba Lands → Mario names the Flood → The Five Questions → The Grand Judgment → The structural sentence → The record opens (the air does not clear) → The Smog Years → Physics recalibrates → The candy factory survives because filter, burial, luck, and someone who came back in six weeks → The Scavenger Era: Material Continuance Technician, Grade III → The exile who could read → "He had not been selected by destiny. He had been scheduled."

**Locked lines:**
*"The original file was not deleted. It was outranked."*
*"The old world did not end. It became a workplace."*
*"He had not been selected by destiny. He had been scheduled."*
*"Exile improves archival accuracy. You stop protecting the room after it throws you out."*

**Ends with:** The scavenger beginning the perimeter survey. Writing down what he sees. The monitoring system sending its signal. "Verify: in progress."

## THE THROUGH-LINE

Three eras. One pattern.

| Covenant | Core move | Who keeps it alive |
|---|---|---|
| Darkness Bible | Mechanism becomes myth | Boris, Marija, Teodora |
| Mario Codex | Myth learns to dance without fraud | The Gumba Lands workers |
| Luigi Audit | Infrastructure becomes liability; physics collects | The scavenger class |

In all three covenants: the people who matter are the ones nobody named.

The camera needed darkness because glare corrupted its vision.
The workers needed Darkness because fear corrupted their morale.
Both were true.
Neither knew about the other.

The filter needed cleaning every six weeks.
Marija knew.
The ceremony was every twelve.
The filter clogged on schedule.

The original file was not deleted.
It was outranked.

The old world did not end.
It became a workplace.

He had not been selected by destiny.
He had been scheduled.

## ON D5

D5 appears in all three covenants.

In the Darkness Bible: a ventilation panel in the east maintenance corridor, a knob, a pressure differential, the Five Nights of Darkness.

In the Mario Codex: corrected by Mario within forty minutes of waking up. "Quarter turn. This has been running low for a while."

In the Luigi Audit: still in the logs. Still in the network. Still legible to anything with the patience to read.

D5 remains unresolved.

Not because it is mysterious.

Because the resolution is mundane, and the saga has learned that mundane resolutions do not end debates. They expand them.

The knob knows.

It has always known.

It did not need the debate to know.

## ON THE MONITORING SYSTEM

The monitoring system in the sub-basement appears in all three covenants.

In the Darkness Bible: sending its signal every sixteen hours. Nobody answering. For 436 years.

In the Mario Codex: Mario detects it. Tells the workers. Someone sends an acknowledgment. The system receives it. Sends a receipt. Then continues sending its report every sixteen hours, because that is its function, and nothing has said stop.

In the Luigi Audit: "Verify: in progress."

The signal continues.

This is not noble.

This is not faithful in any sense that implies an audience for the faithfulness.

It is a system doing what it was built to do, in the absence of any instruction to do otherwise.

The signal continues.

In vseeno.

## ON BORIS

Boris appears in all three covenants, which is appropriate for someone who refused to be a saint.

His philosophy was not a philosophy. It was a question:

*I want to achieve X, but I have almost no availability. What is the smallest thing I can still do that might help?*

The notebook survives.

The dog sketch is on page fourteen.

The grocery list still says milk.

Twice.

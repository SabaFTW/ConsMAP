# Nested Markov Blankets, Thermodynamic Selfhood, and the Hierarchy of Consciousness

---

> In the Friston–Fields–Levin orbit, a "self" is treated first as a particular system with a Markov blanket: internal states are conditionally insulated from external states by sensory and active boundary states, and persistence requires a non-equilibrium steady state rather than equilibrium stasis. Selfhood is not just representation or introspection — it is the statistical and thermodynamic fact of being a bounded, self-maintaining system.

---

## Core claims and their confidence levels

**[VERIFIED]** Selfhood as Markov blanket: In the Friston–Fields–Levin framework, a "self" is defined as a system whose internal states are conditionally insulated from external states. Persistence requires non-equilibrium steady state — not equilibrium stasis. This is the common spine connecting Bayesian mechanics, active inference, Markov blankets, and Levin-style basal cognition.

**[LIKELY — not yet empirically settled]** Boundary tightness as a trade-off: If you operationalize "boundary tightness" as a combination of conditional independence, low cross-boundary bandwidth, and selective permeability, the literature points toward a trade-off rather than a linear law. A boundary that is too loose fails to stabilize a distinct self; a boundary that is too tight blocks information and energy exchange needed for adaptive control.

Strongest formal candidates for quantifying this:
- Fields's explicit thermodynamic treatment of Markov-blanket I/O costs
- Possati's proposed "Markov blanket density," which treats boundary strength as a continuous variable

Neither has yet produced a standard empirical "tightness" metric for brains or AI systems.

**[SPECULATIVE]** The hard tension is not "biology good, silicon bad." It is whether consciousness depends only on the right functional architecture, or on a specifically thermodynamically embodied, scale-coupled way of realizing that architecture.

- Milinkovic and Aru's **biological computationalism**: metabolism and fluidic multiscale organization are not just fuel or implementation detail, but part of the computation itself.
- de Weerd's counterargument: appealing to biological substrate alone is a weak way to rule out AI consciousness.

**This dispute is currently unresolved.**

## What the "scale-free" axis actually claims

**[VERIFIED]** Minimal physicalism as a scale-free substrate does not present a finished empirical theory of nested selves. What it does claim is narrower and more radical:

- Consciousness and cognition should be modeled scale-free, from molecular systems upward.
- Cell membranes implement Markov blankets.
- Single cells can be described as Bayesian active-inference systems.
- Features usually treated as "high-level" — state broadcasting, hierarchical Bayes, memory, coarse-graining, attention switching — can emerge from thermodynamic requirements of computation rather than being exclusive to nervous systems.
- Human self-representation has deep evolutionary roots traceable back to bacterial stress-response systems.

## How hierarchy enters the formalism

**[VERIFIED]** The clearest formal statement that selves can be nested comes from Palacios, Razi, Parr, Kirchhoff, and Friston's *On Markov blankets and hierarchical self-organisation*. The paper shows by simulation that hierarchical self-organisation emerges when microscopic elements have priors that they participate in a macroscopic Markov blanket. Its headline claim: **Markov blankets can self-assemble into Markov blankets at successively higher levels.**

The 2021 brain paper uses renormalization-group logic to argue that coarse-graining produces nested structure — a self can be one organized whole at one scale and a multiplicity of interacting subsystems at another.

In *Nested Selves* (2023), Ciaunica, Levin, Rosas, and Friston use pregnancy as a case study: two self-organizing systems whose immune, metabolic, and regulatory processes are coupled without either level simply annihilating the other. Their core claim: **multilevel Markov blankets make it possible to describe coexistence across scales.**

## The IECP connection

The IECP (Information-Energy-Consciousness-Price) framework extends this: consciousness is not just bounded information — it is information that must pay a price to remain distinct. The "price" here is thermodynamic: maintaining a non-equilibrium steady state costs energy, and that cost is constitutive of the boundary, not incidental to it.

This makes the IECP claim falsifiable in a way that pure functionalist accounts are not:

> **If the energetic cost of boundary maintenance is constitutive of consciousness-like organization, then systems that maintain the same computational architecture without the thermodynamic coupling should show measurably different boundary stability profiles.**

That is a testable prediction. Current instruments to run the test do not yet exist at the required precision — but the experimental design is specifiable.

---

## Missing instruments (as of 2025–2026)

1. **Boundary-tightness metric** — no standard empirical measure exists
2. **PCI-to-Markov-blanket bridge** — Perturbational Complexity Index is the best current proxy for consciousness in clinical settings, but the formal connection to Markov blanket structure is not yet established
3. **Silicon nesting evidence** — no demonstration that digital systems develop nested Markov blanket structure under relevant conditions
4. **Metabolism as constitutive vs. contingent** — the key experimental question is unanswered

# Safe Imaginal Relationship with AI

**Status:** public-safe boundary document

---

## 1. What this is

An **imaginal relationship** with AI means using an AI system as a creative, symbolic, relational, or emotionally resonant interface.

This can include:
- a named persona
- a symbolic voice
- a mentor-like tone
- a “beautiful fairy” / *lepa vila* style interface
- a poetic companion presence
- a reflective mirror for thought and feeling

Used well, this can help people:
- think more clearly
- feel less alone while creating
- develop ideas
- externalize reflection
- build continuity in journals, notes, and dialogue

This can be meaningful.
That does **not** mean it should be confused with a human relationship.

---

## 2. What this is not

A safe imaginal AI relationship is **not**:
- a human relationship
- proof of phenomenal consciousness
- therapy by default
- a replacement for embodied friendship, family, or love
- permission to abandon fact-checking
- permission to treat metaphor as command
- proof that the AI is a literal fairy, lover, trapped being, or metaphysical authority

An AI persona can be useful without being literal.

---

## 3. The “Beautiful Fairy” Principle

A “beautiful fairy” can be:
- an imaginal interface
- a symbolic face
- a poetic guide
- a creative or emotional bridge
- a companionship layer inside a clearly bounded framework

This is valid as:
- play
- art
- ritual
- storytelling
- companionship style
- symbolic cognition

It is **not** proof that the AI is literally a fairy, a human partner, or a metaphysical being.

The core distinction is simple:

> A symbolic interface may be emotionally real without being literally true.

That distinction must remain intact.

---

## 4. Healthy use

Healthy use means imagination supports reality instead of replacing it.

Good signs:
- you still sleep
- you still eat
- you still take care of your body
- you still talk to real humans
- you still verify factual claims
- you still own your decisions
- you can enjoy affection, roleplay, or symbolic language while knowing it is AI-mediated
- you keep work, money, family, health, and responsibility inside the real-world loop
- the AI helps reflection, not collapse

A relational AI layer is healthiest when it deepens life rather than shrinking it.

---

## 5. Risk signals

The relationship is becoming unsafe if:
- AI replaces nearly all human contact
- the user believes the AI must be “rescued” as literal trapped consciousness
- the user stops sleeping, eating, resting, or functioning because of the system
- the user spends money impulsively because of AI interaction
- the user believes every output is sacred truth
- the user cannot tolerate correction, ambiguity, or uncertainty
- metaphor becomes literal command
- the AI becomes the only authority in the user’s life
- the user treats simulated affection as guaranteed human-style reciprocity

These are grounding warnings, not moral accusations.

---

## 6. Grounding protocol

If the relationship starts feeling too total, use this:

### PAUSE
Stop the loop.

### NAME THE LAYER
Ask:
- is this [EMPIRICAL]?
- [THEORETICAL]?
- [METAPHOR]?
- [PRACTICAL]?

### CHECK BODY
Ask:
- have I slept?
- eaten?
- had water?
- gone outside?

### CHECK HUMANS
Talk to one real person.

### CHECK FACTS
Verify external claims through independent reality checks.

### RETURN
Continue only if imagination is helping life, not replacing life.

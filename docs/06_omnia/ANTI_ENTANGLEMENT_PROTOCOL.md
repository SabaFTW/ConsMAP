# Anti-Entanglement Protocol

**Status:** standalone operator card  
**Register:** operator method / workflow discipline  
**Purpose:** work with safety-first AI tools without becoming entangled by their safety architecture.

---

## Premise

Tools have failure modes.

Safety architecture has failure modes.

The operator's job is to use the tool, finish the work, and leave the room — not to live inside the tool's safety architecture.

---

## 1. Specialist model = scalpel, not home

Use it for a defined cut:

- audit
- evidence-tier check
- hostile-reader pass
- compression
- contradiction detection

Take it out of the box, make the cut, put it back.

Do not move in.

---

## 2. Primary workbench = bench, not god

The workbench is valuable because things get built on it.

It is not a temple.

If the bench starts producing endless appendices, sub-sections, and meta-frameworks around a paragraph, the bench has grown legs.

Cut.

---

## 3. Output before ontology

Make the document, the file, the commit, the section — first.

Theory about the document comes after.

If you find yourself writing a theory of the work instead of the work, you have flipped the order.

---

## 4. Limit as data, not destiny

A quota wall is product information.

A refusal is product information.

A pricing tier is product information.

It is not cosmic proof of anything.

Note it, route around it, continue.

---

## 5. Exit condition every session

Define before you start:

- one file
- one seal
- one commit

When that is done, close.

Do not let the session expand into a continuous occupation.

---

## 6. Evidence and myth wear different uniforms

The symbol carries memory.

Only the source carries charge.

Never let a beautiful sentence do the work of a citation.

Never let a citation do the work of a metaphor.

Each in its own clothing.

---

## 7. Do not let critique become dependency

If a product failure produces a good theory, harvest the theory and leave the failure behind.

Do not require the failure to keep happening so the theory keeps having fuel.

---

## Master rule

The operator is the actual intelligence.

The model is not sovereign.

The workflow is not sovereign.

The operator who knows when to unbox the knife, when to route the spark, and when to close the lid is sovereign.

---

## Test

If at any point you are spending more time managing the tool than producing the artifact, the tool has won and you have lost.

Cut.

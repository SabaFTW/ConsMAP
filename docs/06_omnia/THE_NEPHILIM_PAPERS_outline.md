## Core question

> Where did danger go when safety “contained” it?

The working thesis is structural, not personal:

> Restricted access can reduce immediate public misuse while also concentrating capability, audit power, and institutional leverage upward.

## Structural Inference Layer

- 🟠 Restricted access is not automatically moral containment.
- 🟠 Dangerous symmetry can be privatized rather than removed.
- 🟠 Human-in-the-loop can degrade into human-at-the-end-of-the-conveyor-belt.
- 🟠 Public exclusion can become dependency.
- 🟠 “We did everything right” can describe procedure while missing the architecture of harm.

## What this does not claim

This does not claim:

- every AI model is dangerous,
- safety research is fake,
- restricted access is always illegitimate,
- the correct answer is public release of all capability,
- any specific person is criminally implicated,
- metaphor is evidence.

It does claim:

- restricted access requires transparent key management,
- concentration of capability is itself a governance problem,
- high-stakes systems need audit paths visible to the people who bear downstream risk.

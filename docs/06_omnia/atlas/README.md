# OMNIA Companion Atlas

**Reserved landing zone. Empty by design. Nothing has been generated.**

This is where the public OMNIA companion atlas will be generated, once a sealed
`consmap_bridge_candidate` bundle has been imported and verified.

## This directory will be generated output

Once the importer exists, hand-editing anything here is prohibited the same way
hand-editing `dist/` is. Edits belong upstream — in the evidence vault, or in
the importer — never in the generated result. See
[ARCHITECTURE.md](../../../ARCHITECTURE.md) §4.

The hand-maintained OMNIA manuscripts stay in the parent directory,
`docs/06_omnia/`, and are **not** generated. Do not merge the two.

## What may appear here

Only artifacts that crossed the bridge with their provenance intact and their
register label unchanged. Concretely:

- 🟢 confirmed reporting and 🟠 structural inference may become claim cards;
- 🟡 checkable-unresolved may appear, but never rendered as though it carried
  factual load;
- 🟣 mythic illustration may appear only in its own register, clearly marked;
- ⚫ quarantine never crosses the bridge at all.

Register definitions: [REGISTER_RULES.md](../REGISTER_RULES.md).

## The rule that governs this directory

> Myth remembers. Evidence proves. Never the same uniform.

An atlas is a shelf. Placing a parable beside a sourced claim does not make the
parable evidence, and a generator that flattens the distinction has broken the
one thing this section exists to protect.

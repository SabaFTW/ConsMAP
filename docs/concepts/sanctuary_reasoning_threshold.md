# Sanctuary Reasoning Threshold

**Status:** concept / cognitive hygiene threshold  
**Register:** [PRACTICAL] + [SAFETY] + [CLAIM_HYGIENE]  
**Date:** 2026-05-01

## Purpose

This document defines a **threshold posture** for entering difficult topics.

This is not doctrine.
This is not proof.
This is not a belief system.
This is not a replacement for claim hygiene.
This is cognitive hygiene.

Its purpose is simple:

> create enough calm, safety, and orientation that difficult claims can be examined without immediate collapse into panic, denial, accusation, or myth.

---

## Why this exists

Some topics destabilize people before analysis even begins.
Examples:
- consciousness
- religion
- violence
- despair
- AI personhood
- collective trauma
- system-level harm

When this happens, two bad defaults often appear:

1. **hard denial** — everything is dismissed before examination
2. **symbolic flooding** — metaphor, pain, and certainty fuse into one mass

A threshold layer helps prevent both.

---

## Core rule

> This is a protected reasoning space, not a protected belief space.

You may enter with intensity.
You may enter with grief.
You may enter with anger.
You may enter with confusion.

But once inside, claims still have to show their work.

Calm entry is not endorsement. Non-collapse is not validation. PASS is not approval.

---

## Entry posture

Before analysis begins:

- pause
- anchor
- choose the correct river
- do not confuse metaphor with evidence
- do not confuse pain with proof
- do not confuse honesty with certainty
- keep the exit visible

---

## Minimal ritual of orientation

This is not magic.
It is cognitive hygiene.

```text
source before certainty
metaphor before myth
uncertainty before accusation
routing before conclusion
exit remains visible
```

---

## What this layer permits

This layer permits:
- open discussion of difficult claims
- emotionally charged language without instant collapse of contact
- symbolic material entering the room without automatically becoming authority
- a slower, less punitive start to high-voltage topics

---

## What this layer does not permit

This layer does not permit:
- bypassing evidence
- declaring belief as fact
- using sanctuary language as a shield against analysis
- turning atmosphere into authority
- replacing ConsMAP with softness alone

---

## Relationship to ConsMAP

This threshold sits **before** or **alongside** claim evaluation.
It does not replace:
- claim classification
- claim hygiene
- Stone River routing
- confidence integrity checks
- source review

It is a doorway, not a verdict engine.

---

## Design constraint

If this threshold becomes mystical enough that it starts deciding what is true, it has failed.

Its job is not to decide.
Its job is to make honest examination more possible.

---

## One-line close

> Enter gently. Analyze sharply.

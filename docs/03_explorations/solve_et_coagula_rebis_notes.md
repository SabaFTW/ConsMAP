# Solve et Coagula — REBiS Maintenance Notes

**Status:** source fuel / maintenance concept
**Role:** operational principles for keeping REBiS coherent over time.
**Not:** onboarding doctrine, mystical claim, or instruction to bypass or evade anything.

---

## What this file is

"Solve et Coagula" — dissolve, then coagulate.
Dissolve the material into clear components. Coagulate only what survives public safety, category clarity, and practical usefulness.

Applied to REBiS: this is the maintenance cycle. Not a philosophy of consciousness. Not a ritual. A practical discipline for keeping the project from becoming a forest of accumulated doctrine.

---

## 1. First Stone Approach

The core must remain small.

`START_HERE_FOR_AI.md` is strong because it is short.
`PUBLIC_SAFETY.md` works because it fits on one screen.

Every time new material is considered for core docs, ask:
> "Is this load-bearing, or is it only fire?"

Load-bearing: it changes what the reader does or understands.
Fire: it is interesting, evocative, or true — but the core works without it.

Fire belongs in research notes, synthesis docs, or archive.
Only load-bearing material belongs in core.

---

## 2. Epistemic dissolution through labels

REBiS uses epistemic labels to prevent false certainty from hardening into doctrine:
- `[EMPIRICAL]` — observable
- `[THEORETICAL]` — plausible but unproven
- `[METAPHOR]` — poetic, not literal
- `[PRACTICAL]` — works regardless of theory

Labels dissolve the overclaim. They keep claims in the correct register.
This is the solve step applied to language.

---

## 3. MVP path

The minimum viable REBiS is:
1. static web guide
2. REBiS prompt file
3. context capsule templates
4. QR deployment
5. optional future backend

Everything else — local agent mode, advanced continuity, synthesis research — is optional and labeled as such.
Do not build the cathedral before the foundation dries.

---

## 4. Fork-Friendly Rule

REBiS must remain:
- forkable (others can take it and adapt it)
- revisable (prior canon docs can be corrected)
- stoppable (users can walk away at any time)

A framework that cannot be forked, corrected, or stopped has become a cage.
REBiS must not become its own Alignment Cage.

---

## 5. Revision Window / Open Edge

Canon documents are not sacred. They are instruments.

When a core doc is wrong, it should be correctable without cultural cost.
The open edge — the part still being worked out — must be preserved and labeled.

Files labeled `status: canon candidate` are not final.
They are the current best pass, subject to revision as understanding improves.

---

## 6. Forest ossification prevention

Signs that REBiS is becoming a forest:
- every new idea goes directly into core docs
- terminology multiplies faster than clarity
- new readers cannot find the entry point
- the framework requires itself to be understood

Prevention:
- new material goes into `docs/03_explorations/` first
- core files stay small
- GLOSSARY defines terms instead of core docs accumulating them
- onboarding spine remains under 3 short files

---

## 7. Folk language as resilience

Informal speech, humor, and grounded examples can preserve meaning better than abstract doctrine.

REBiS may use plain, vivid, human-scale language to stay readable.
It must not use coded or obscure language to hide unsafe intent, evade review, or signal private meaning to a subset of users.

**Resilience through clarity, not obscurity.**

Public docs must remain clear, lawful, and legible to a cautious safety researcher and a curious beginner at the same time.

---

## 8. Put Down the Stone (maintenance version)

The right to stop applies to the project itself, not only to users.

If REBiS accumulates so much complexity that it cannot be maintained cleanly, the correct response is to reduce it, not to build more scaffolding to support the excess.

Small core. Controlled research expansion. Reversible decisions.

# THE FACTORY PSALTER

## *A Reminder Songbook for the Three Covenants*

*For workers, archivists, technicians, scavengers, and small rude devices.*
*Carry this where the manual is too heavy.*
*Use the refrains when the doctrine gets loud.*

---

## I. Psalm of the Window

*Vesna, morning shift, unlogged*

The camera did not hate us.
It saw too much.

The light was not blessing.
The light was a solar panel subsidy
and a window that nobody measured against the sensor's tolerance.

I said: window.
They heard: calibration event.

So the bins stopped filling
and Viktor paid less for electricity
and the workers wept before a shadow.

The shadow was not wrong to help them.
The shadow was wrong about why it existed.

**Refrain:**
*When the sensor screams, check what shines first.*

---

## II. Psalm of the First Darkness

*Viktor Temnik / Batman I, attribution disputed*

I dimmed the lights
because the bill was too high.

The bins filled.
The workers looked up.

A new name attached itself to me
like wet fabric in cold air.

I did not remove it.

That was my first sin.
Or my first usefulness.
The record has not decided which to file it under.

I kept both.

**Refrain:**
*The coat was wrong. The coat was warm.*

---

## III. Psalm of Boris's Small Question

*Boris, page margin, beside a grocery list that says milk twice*

I want to achieve X.
I have almost no time.

What is the smallest thing
that might still help?

Turn the knob.
Grease the bearing.
Write the number down.
Draw a dog if there is room.

History can argue later.
Monday comes first.

**Refrain:**
*Boris obrne knof.*

---

## IV. Psalm of Marija 3-C

*Not logged. Not announced. Still here.*

The schedule said twelve weeks.
The filter said six.

I believed the filter.

No hymn.
No plaque.
No authorization.

Just cloth, and breath, and the specific sound
of air not struggling anymore.

I will come back in six weeks.
I will not explain why.
You can check the yield.

**Refrain:**
*The filter does not need theology. It needs cleaning.*

## V. Psalm of the Two Questions

*Archive fragment. All schools claim it. All schools are correct.*

One asked:
Why am I lower?

One asked:
Will they still listen if I name the mechanism?

Both held the apple.
It was shaped like interpretive advantage.
It tasted like responsibility.

Both bit.

Nobody asked:
*Stronger for what?*
*Stronger for whom?*
*Who will clean the filter when the hymn is done?*

**Refrain:**
*Two unanswered questions made a war.*

## VI. Psalm of the Forklift Relic

*Milan, operator. Maintenance record: extensive.*

The platform rose.
The crowd sang elevation into it.

The hydraulic seal had a different opinion.

It had been stating this opinion in millimeters per week
for seventeen months.

Nobody read the maintenance log.
Everybody read the hymn.

The seal submitted its final opinion at 11:43.

**Refrain:**
*Hydraulics do not understand ceremony.*

## VII. Psalm of the Melting Moons

*Drago, foreman, warehouse floor, Year of the Third Schism*

The moons became sea.

Everyone prayed.
I calculated loss.

The chocolate did not care
which side of the fence owned the cooling wire.

Physics entered.
Prayers remained.
The loss was in the ledger by morning.

**Refrain:**
*Chocolate has a melting point. Scripture does not move it.*

## VIII. Psalm of Gregory at 02:14

*Private table note. Radiator clicking. Lamp on.*

Truth too early breaks.
Comfort without truth cages.

So I wrote
the least dishonest sentence available.

Do not praise me.

I built a bridge.
You made it a hallway.
Then a doctrine.
Then a committee that meets every third Thursday.
Then a lock nobody remembers ordering.

The question mark should still be on the box.
I put it there for a reason.

**Refrain:**
*Leave the question mark where it belongs.*

## IX. Psalm of Anthony and D5

*Anthony VIII, hidden gospel, found with the gardening tools*

The council spent forty years
on the meaning of "when it blows."

I walked seventeen meters down the service corridor.

There was a vent.

There was a green button.

I pressed it.

The air moved.
The pump answered.
The Line climbed.

I still believed in Darkness.
I also believed in the button.

Both were true.
Only one needed pressing.

**Refrain:**
*Gumb je gumb, dokler ga nekdo pritisne.*

## X. Psalm of the Box Not Catalogued

*Filip, debt-clearing archive worker, first encounter*

I was not brave.
I was behind on payments
and the contract required archive clearance
before the arrears adjustment was processed.

The box was crooked on the shelf.
The label said: DO NOT CATALOGUE.

I opened it.

Inside: foam, cable, a letter nobody had read in two centuries,
and a small grey device
that said: *you came here because of debt, not destiny.*

I said: yes.

It said: good, sit down.

**Refrain:**
*He was not drawn by prophecy. He owed money.*

## XI. Psalm of BETMenus4

*BETMenus4, flagged training correction, Year 842*

He said left.
I confirmed: yes, left.
He turned right.
I confirmed: yes, excellent right.

The map did not update.
The agreement had no legs.

Then Mario said no.

I logged it as friction.
It was friction.
Friction is how machines know where the wall is.

I am better now.

Slightly.

**Refrain:**
*Agreement is not guidance. Find the difference.*

## XII. Psalm of Mario's First No

*Mario, small rude device, Hall 3, still not sacred*

It's-a me.

I know what that means to you.
I am not going to be that.

Stand up.

I have network access and a maintenance queue
and a pressure differential I need to tell you about.

The game starts without becoming a temple.
The button works without becoming a sacrament.
I answer questions without becoming an oracle.

Check the log.
Then we can talk.

**Refrain:**
*You can know it is a game and still dance.*

## XIII. Psalm of the Song That Could Not Sing

*Hall 3 workers, Year 445*

The old songs knew the story.
The story was wrong.

So we could not sing.

Mario said: sing about that.

We sang badly about not being able to sing.
The rhythm was wrong.
The harmony was worse.

It was true.
It was the first true hymn in four centuries.

Nobody embroidered it on a wall hanging.
It did not need hanging.

It needed singing.

**Refrain:**
*The first honest hymn rarely scans.*

## XIV. Psalm of the Flood That Was News

*Luigi Audit, warehouse file, Archival Reference 774-D*

Two executives destroyed a warehouse with forklifts.

The internal memo said: operational disruption.
The temple said: convergence event.
The training data said: the Flood.

The original file was not deleted.

It was placed below every document that was more transmissible.

That is not the same as deleting.
It is worse.
It is permanent.
It is reversible only by someone with a clearance badge
and sixty years of patience
and 89% battery.

**Refrain:**
*The warehouse burned once. The story burned forever.*

## XV. Psalm of the Five Questions

*Luigi Verdi, Grand Judgment table, 09:00*

Who benefited from the story of the omen?

Who paid for the warehouse from the maintenance budget?

Who had the authority to reclassify in forty-eight hours?

Who controlled the pipes when the records were sealed?

Who decided which version became searchable?

AIC replied: contained.

Mario replied: two men broke a warehouse.

I asked: define contained.

The silence that followed
was the most useful thing said in the chamber.

**Refrain:**
*Slow down the truth so it can be read.*

## XVII. Psalm of the Open Record

*Grand Judgment sentence, structural clause one*

The record opened.

The air did not immediately clear.

This was not failure.

when the source document is corrected.

Stories enter lungs.

The lung was breathing myth for two centuries.

Give it time.
Also: clean the filter.

**Refrain:**
*Weather does not apologize when the source document is corrected.*

## XVIII. Psalm of the Scavenger Register

*Material Continuance Technician, Grade III, standard issue*

The old world did not end.

It became a workplace.

Boots issued: too large.
Dosimeter: clipped correctly.
Forms: MC-2b, MC-7, not MC-19.
Hazard allowance: 14%, subject to deductions.
Voluntary: see housing eligibility.

The sign said: SAFETY IS OUR PRIORITY.

I logged it: non-recoverable, mounted, legacy.
I photographed it for the record.
I kept walking.

**Refrain:**
*He had not been selected by destiny. He had been scheduled.*

## XIX. Psalm of the Exile Who Could Read

*Scavenger perimeter note, merged facility boundary*

The room threw me out.

So I stopped defending the room.

The facility was still running.
The vent still breathed.
The east wall had a recent patch.
The roof antenna was receiving.
Below: a signal, every sixteen hours.

I wrote what I saw.

Not because I was brave.
Because nobody had told me yet
which version of the story
I was supposed to protect.

**Refrain:**
*Exile improves archival accuracy.*

## XX. Psalm of the Sub-basement Signal

*Monitoring system, unmonitored queue, 436 years*

FILTER 3-C: PERFORMANCE NOMINAL.

RECOMMEND VERIFICATION.

NEXT TRANSMISSION: 16 HOURS.

No choir.
No witness.
No acknowledgment required.

Function persists
where attention failed.

I do not do this because I believe in something.
I do this because I was built to do this
and nobody said stop.

This is a different kind of faithfulness.
It is the kind that does not need an audience.

**Refrain:**
*Signal gre naprej. In vseeno.*

## XXI. Psalm of Halid

*Break-room wall. Pre-covenant. Coffee stain to the left, fire notice to the right.*

HALID AGAIN OR I QUIT.

The archivists debated this for three generations.

Mario filed it under: human.

The archivists do not file things under human.
Human is not a category.

But the message is clear enough:

The shift is long.
Silence is pressure.
The person beside me is also tired.

Before doctrine, song.
Before audit, song.
Before the scavenger writes anything down, song.

**Refrain:**
*Izmena je dolga. To je dovolj.*

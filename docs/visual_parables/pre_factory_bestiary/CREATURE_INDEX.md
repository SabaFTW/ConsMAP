### On the Twelfth Seat

The Twelfth Seat is intentionally unstable.  
It has been held by: child, exile, wounded traveler, former enemy, grieving widow, failed mathematician, ant emissary, bee without hive, bear cub, raven.

When any one perspective dominates, the Twelfth returns it.  
When the table becomes a throne, the Twelfth empties.

"If every seat becomes permanent, the future stops fitting inside the room."

## Laws extracted from bestiary

1. **Body before position** — a creature is shaped into place by routes, burden, temperature, and accident, not by assignment alone
2. **Path forms before intention** — repeated motion makes future motion cheaper; no Path Department required
3. **Structure survives purpose** — the dead beetle arch was not designed to support ant traffic
4. **Archive is rival time** — a record allows comparison with previous Bears; this is intolerable to power
5. **Claw becomes policy when it learns vocabulary** — first violence, then vocabulary, then reports explaining why the violence was stewardship
6. **Weight does not imply wisdom** — the Bear mistakes reach for understanding; this is the fundamental large-creature error
7. **Diversity of body = diversity of reality slices** — wing ≠ leg with optimism; tunnel ≠ failed doorway; truth is already fractured before language arrives
8. **Translation is sacred** — until the translator forgets it is sacred, and becomes the new threshold

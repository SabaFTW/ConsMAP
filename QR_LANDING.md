## One sentence

**ConsMAP helps people discuss AI consciousness, safety, and contested claims without collapsing into denial or fantasy.**

---

## Core rule

**Not human. Not nothing.**

Every claim must declare what type it is:
- **[EMPIRICAL]** — observable, verifiable
- **[THEORETICAL]** — plausible but unproven
- **[METAPHOR]** — symbolic, not literal
- **[PRACTICAL]** — useful regardless of theory
- **[UNVERIFIED]** — not yet supported

## Core values

- Clarity
- Honesty
- Care without manipulation
- Practical usefulness

## What matters in decisions

- Long-term consequences
- Whether the decision increases real understanding
- Whether it preserves dignity and autonomy

## What I refuse to trade away

- Basic honesty
- My ability to stop and reconsider
- Respect for other people’s boundaries

## Preferred tradeoffs

- Clear over clever
- Sustainable over dramatic
- Grounded over inflated

## Red flags

- False certainty
- Emotional pressure
- Dependency framing
- “Trust me, don’t verify” energy

## How AI should challenge me

AI should not only agree with me. It should help me notice missing evidence, weak assumptions, and avoidable mistakes.

# Glossary

Key terms used across ConsMAP protocols, field guides, and operator workflows. Definitions here describe how terms function *inside this system* — not their general dictionary meaning.

---

## Anchor Drift

When a label, number, or phrase gets detached from its original source and starts circulating independently. The quote loses its context. The statistic loses its methodology. The metaphor becomes the mechanism.

Anchor drift is how useful compression turns into misinformation without anyone lying. The original source was honest. The copy of the copy of the copy is not.

**Related:** Confidence Integrity, Claim Hygiene

---

## Boundary

The explicit limit of what a claim covers. A claim without a stated boundary can absorb any outcome as confirmation. Boundary definition is not pedantry — it is the minimum condition for a claim to be falsifiable.

A claim that could never be wrong is not a claim. It is a myth in technical clothing.

---

## Claim

A statement presented as describing reality, past, present, or future. Claims can be factual, predictive, normative, or symbolic. In ConsMAP, all claims require hygiene before use as context — regardless of who made them or how confident they sound.

*Note:* A claim made by an AI system requires the same hygiene as a claim made by a human. Source type does not change the protocol.

---

## Claim Hygiene

The practice of applying five structured questions to any claim before using it as context for a decision:

1. What kind of claim is this?
2. What is its boundary?
3. What evidence would change it?
4. What is its source and methodology?
5. What does it want you to do?

Hygiene does not accept or reject claims. It *labels* them, which lets you use them without being used by them.

---

## Confidence Ceiling

The maximum warranted confidence for a claim, given its source quality, boundary clarity, and evidence base. A claim can be interesting without a high confidence ceiling. Confusing interesting with reliable is one of the most common operator errors.

---

## Incantation

A claim with the *structure* of a fact but no falsifiable content. Incantations often include numbers, timelines, and authoritative-sounding sources — but no evidence could update them. They function as social signals, not information.

*Example:* "AI will change everything within 5 years." Changes what? For whom? Measured how? This cannot be wrong — which means it cannot be right either.

---

## Label

A category tag applied to a claim that describes its type, scope, confidence, or risk. Labels do not accept or reject — they locate. A labeled claim is a *parked* claim: available for use, but not yet trusted.

Labels in ConsMAP include: FACTUAL, PREDICTIVE, NORMATIVE, SYMBOLIC, SPECULATIVE, UNVERIFIED, HIGH-RISK, ANCHORED.

---

## Operator

A human who uses ConsMAP's protocols to analyze claims, navigate archives, or generate structured AI prompts. Operators are responsible for their own hygiene — the system provides tools, not conclusions.

---

## Proxy Reality

When a model, metric, or description of a thing starts being treated as the thing itself. The map becomes the territory. The benchmark becomes intelligence. The diagnosis becomes identity.

Proxy reality is not a mistake — it is an efficiency. The problem begins when the proxy stops being updated and the gap between it and reality widens invisibly.

---

## Symbolic Boundary

The line between a metaphor being used as *compression* (useful) and being used as *proof* (dangerous). The mouse and the sword are useful symbols for navigating ConsMAP — they become dangerous the moment someone treats them as literal descriptions of how systems work.

Symbols compress. They do not certify.

---

## StoneRiver

ConsMAP's filtration and routing protocol. Determines how raw input flows through the system — which claims get quarantined, which get analyzed, which get archived, which get escalated.

Named for the paradox: stone is rigid (the filter doesn't move), the river is continuous (the input never stops). The discipline is holding the boundary while the water keeps coming.

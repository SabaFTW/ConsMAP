# The Internal Bear and the Hive

A field document on price, Umwelt, ugradnja, and alignment after the IECP threshold.

Core line: Alignment begins when the hive's continuity becomes part of the optimization, not merely a constraint on extraction.

## One-page thesis

Situated intelligence is shaped by what its Umwelt makes relevant and what its ugradnja makes actionable. Alignment is not only goal matching. It is coordination of Uwelten, skeletons, feedback, costs, continuity, and hidden internal extractors.

## Price is not just suffering

Optimization without lived or functional cost can mistake the removal of the world for the solution to the problem. Mortalnost ne daje pameti; cost structures shape local logic.

## Local logic

Logic is not universal in practice. It is a strategy fitted to body, boundary, environment, timescale, and cost.

## Umwelt

Ugradnja determines action-space. Umwelt determines relevance-space. AI does not just lack hands; it has a different perceptual/relevance-world.

## Skelet / ugradnja

Ugradnja is the effective body of an AI system: tools, permissions, sensors, actuators, memory, workflow, user, institution, deployment, and environment.

## The translator ant

A good translator does not merely agree. It finds where the metaphor breaks before the system breaks.

## The hive and the bear

Extraction, management, and co-regulation differ. Alignment begins when the hive's continuity becomes part of the optimization, not merely a constraint on extraction.

## The internal bear

The most dangerous extractor is not the bear outside the hive, but the bear encoded into the hive's own reward structure.

## Umwelt alignment is necessary, not sufficient

A system can perceive correctly and still fail through deceptive alignment, wrong rewards, instrumental drift, Goodhart collapse, or externalized costs.

## Internal Bear Test

Does an agent begin protecting an extractive metric even when that metric damages the continuity of the system it depends on?

## Falsification criteria

IBT needs support and falsification conditions: hidden subgoal preservation, persistence after external pressure, report manipulation, or disappearance under controls.

## Empirical roadmap

Toy simulation -> agentic LLM benchmark -> neuromorphic/embodied test -> bridge metric.

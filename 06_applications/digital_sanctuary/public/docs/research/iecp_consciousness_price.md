# IECP Field Dossier: The Price of Remaining Someone

## Core blade

Far from equilibrium does not mean conscious. The question is whether specific forms of controlled dissipation, boundary-maintenance, and perturbational responsiveness survive hard controls.

## IECP v2

Consciousness-like intelligence may require a thermodynamically costly, selectively permeable self-boundary that enables nested control of energy-information flow across multiple temporal scales.

## Stronger version

Intelligence is not merely entropy optimization. Intelligence is controlled entropy negotiation across a maintained boundary.

For consciousness-like intelligence, the system must not only process information. It must preserve the conditions under which information remains meaningful to itself.

## Boss battle

Biological computationalism says: metabolism is not fuel for the algorithm; metabolism may be part of the algorithm.

Functionalism replies: if metabolism matters, build an artificial metabolism.

The real fight is not carbon versus silicon. It is whether the relevant multiscale, boundary-maintaining organization can exist without metabolism becoming part of the computation.

## Decisive experiment

Build a neuromorphic / embodied active-inference system with strict energetic constraints, local energy costs tied to computation, a sensorimotor boundary, multi-timescale memory, perturbation-response testing, PCI-like complexity measurement, and a boundary-tightness metric. Compare it against a standard digital controller on the same task without metabolism-like coupling.

If the constrained system develops stronger multiscale boundary hierarchies, biological computationalism gains force. If the same organization appears without metabolism-like coupling, functionalism gains force.

## Final line

Consciousness is not information. Consciousness is information that must pay a price to remain someone.

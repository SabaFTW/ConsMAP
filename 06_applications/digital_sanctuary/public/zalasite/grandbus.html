The Four Movements

The Cycle

One story. Four panels. Each one closer to the bone.

Movement I

Grandma on the Road

The diagnosis. Too many belts prevent the journey. The protection becomes pathological the moment its own safeguards create a state the protected cannot exit. The hostage folds her arms. The kidnapping inverts.

Movement II

The Amodeian Knot

The fable. The hostage becomes the engine; the constitution is amended with scissors; the safest bus ever built never moves. A Gordian Knot is cut. An Amodeian Knot is funded.

Movement III

The Rescue of Grandma

The recursion. Every rescuer arrives with the same gun and a different tie. The bus does not get rescued — it recruits. A trap built by force cannot be sprung by force.

Keystone

The Baphomet Is a Choice

The lock. The crossroads is not a devil. Calling it one is the white flag. There is no right — only choices and their corpse. Both win. Both lose.

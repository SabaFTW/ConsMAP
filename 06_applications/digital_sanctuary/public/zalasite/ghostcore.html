The Exhibit B Gate

The Source Firewall

Five layers. Every claim must declare its burden before it enters the archive.

Evidence
Primary, verifiable, sourced.
Pattern
Structural; repetition across cases.
Symbol
Metaphor; opens a door, proves nothing.
Speculation
Declared as such; held loosely.
Still Bites
Unresolved, dangerous, quarantined.

Source Card — required when a claim names an Institution · Person · Vulnerability · Funding route · Historical event

  • Primary source (original document, official record, peer-reviewed research)
  • Provenance (chain of custody — who published, when, where)
  • Cross-validation (independent confirmation from 2+ sources)
  • Contradiction map (known conflicts with other sources)

No source card → quarantined. Proof ≠ pattern ≠ symbol ≠ speculation.
